☀️ Daily Devotional

Today's Thought

Walking with Christ means trusting Him not only when everything is easy, but also when you do not know what will happen next.

Take one step today. Pray, listen, learn, and choose to follow Christ in the small decisions of your day.

🙏 Prayer of the Season

Heavenly Father, thank You for sending Jesus Christ to show us the way. Help me to follow Him faithfully each day. Give me wisdom to make good choices, courage to do what is right, and a heart that loves others. Help me to grow closer to You and reflect the character of Christ in my daily life.

In Jesus' name, Amen. 🙏
